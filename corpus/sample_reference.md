# Aurora Research Station Reference

This fictional reference document is the permanent sample corpus for the UI test suite.

## Instrument checkout

Researchers may borrow instruments for up to 7 days. Instruments must be returned to the equipment desk by 17:00 on the final day. Extension requests must be approved before the original return date.

## Observation room

The observation room accommodates up to 12 people. Reservations are available from 09:00 to 18:00. Each reservation may last up to 2 hours.

## Equipment support

Equipment support is available at the station service desk from 09:00 to 17:00 on weekdays. Faulty instruments must be returned to the service desk and must not be used until inspected.
