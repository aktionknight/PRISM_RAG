# PRISM RAG Test Suite Corpus

## Venue A (Pune Workshop Center)
The Pune Workshop Center is a premier venue for mid-sized events.
- **Capacity**: The primary workshop hall holds up to 30 attendees. The secondary hall holds up to 15 attendees.
- **Cancellation Policy**: Cancellations made more than 14 days before the event receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 14 days are non-refundable.
- **Catering Options**: We offer on-site catering ranging from standard coffee breaks to full three-course meals. External catering is also permitted with a 10% surcharge.

## Venue B (Mumbai Grand Hall)
The Mumbai Grand Hall caters to large corporate events.
- **Capacity**: The main hall holds up to 500 attendees. 
- **Cancellation Policy**: Cancellations must be made 30 days in advance for a 50% refund.
- **Catering Options**: All catering must be handled by our exclusive in-house partner. External food is strictly prohibited.
